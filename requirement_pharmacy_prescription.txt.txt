Pharmacy & Prescription Management Doctors prescribe medication after consultation. Pharmacy fulfills prescriptions. Tracks:
Inventory
Dispensing
Refills
Why this matters: right now there's no record of what's been prescribed, and pharmacy has no visibility into what needs fulfilling. Prescriptions should be linked to patients and referrals, support multiple medications per prescription, and record dispensing activity. Inventory levels should be updated as medications are dispensed, with low-stock items clearly identified.
