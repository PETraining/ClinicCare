Patient Portal Instead of clinician-facing software, build something for patients. Entirely different frontend. Patient can:
View referrals
View appointments
Download documents
Upload pre-visit forms
Complete questionnaires
Track referral progress
Why this matters: patients currently have no visibility into their own care — no way to check status or get information to us before they arrive. The portal should present only information relevant to the logged-in patient, provide a simple and intuitive experience, and allow information submitted by patients (such as forms and questionnaires) to become available to clinicians before the appointment.
