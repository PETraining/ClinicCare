Insurance Eligibility & Prior Authorization Given a referral, 
determine whether insurance requires prior authorization before a specialist visit, 
and track status: Not Required / Pending / Approved / Denied. Why this matters: 
we're seeing denied claims because prior authorization wasn't confirmed before the visit happened — staff need this visible
 before the appointment, not discovered after. The solution should allow insurance information to be associated with a patient,
  capture authorization requests and decisions, and make the current authorization status visible wherever referrals and appointments are viewed.
