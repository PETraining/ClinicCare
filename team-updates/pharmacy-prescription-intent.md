# Pharmacy & Prescription Management - Feature Intent

## What Is This Actually For?

Enable doctors to prescribe medications electronically within the referral workflow, track prescriptions through pharmacy fulfillment, and manage pharmacy inventory with real-time dispensing and low-stock alerts.

## Who Needs It?

**Doctors** — Need to issue prescriptions electronically instead of paper notes.

**Pharmacies** — Need visibility into incoming prescriptions to prepare stock and track what's being dispensed.

**Patients** — Need to know when their prescription is ready for pickup and see their medication history.

## Why?

Currently, there's no record of what medications are prescribed after a referral is completed. Pharmacies operate blind without visibility into incoming prescriptions. No one tracks inventory levels, dispensing activity, or can identify low-stock medications.

This feature closes that gap by creating an electronic prescription workflow that connects doctors → pharmacies → patients, with complete inventory visibility and audit trails.
