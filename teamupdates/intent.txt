
Intent: Insurance Prior Authorization Tracking

Before scheduling a specialist appointment, check if the patient's insurance company requires prior authorization.
 If they do, get that approval *before* the visit. If you schedule the visit without approval, 
 the insurance will deny the claim and the clinic doesn't get paid.

Who needs it:
Clinic staff scheduling appointments and managing referrals.

Why:
Right now, staff book appointments first and find out too late that authorization was needed. 
By then, it's too late to get approval. This feature makes authorization status visible upfront,
 so staff see the requirement before scheduling.

What it does:
- Link patient insurance information to their record
- Request authorization from the insurance company when needed
- Track whether authorization is: Not Required / Pending / Approved / Denied
- Show the status when staff view referrals and appointments
